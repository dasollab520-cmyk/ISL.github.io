Intelligent Systems Laboratory

The Intelligent Systems Laboratory develops technologies that emulate human intellectual abilities such as learning, estimation, adaptation, and evolution, and applies them across a wide range of fields. Building on soft computing based intelligent algorithms, our ultimate goals include developing intelligent chips that bring intelligence to hardware, realizing digital eco-systems through their applications, and optimizing semiconductor manufacturing processes with intelligent algorithms.

Professor Kim's research focuses on intelligent semiconductor manufacturing and BIT fusion technology based on intelligent algorithms and statistical analysis. He received his Ph.D. from the Georgia Institute of Technology (Georgia Tech) in 1998 for research on the modeling, optimization, and control of MCM (multi-chip module) packaging processes using neural networks and evolutionary algorithms. He then worked as a research associate at the Packaging Research Center (PRC), Georgia Tech, and as a senior research engineer at the SoC Team, System LSI Business, Samsung Electronics. Since 2001, he has led the Intelligent Systems Laboratory at the School of Information, Communications and Electronics Engineering, The Catholic University of Korea, with research funding from the Small and Medium Business Administration, the Korea Sanhak Foundation, ETRI, the Ministry of Knowledge Economy, the Ministry of Science and ICT, Micron Technology (U.S.A.), and the National Research Foundation of Korea.
